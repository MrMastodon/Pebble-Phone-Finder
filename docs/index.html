PhoneFinder

Ring your phone from your Pebble watch — loud, even when it’s on silent.

Most find-my-phone watchapps play a media sound at whatever volume happens to be set, which is no help if the phone is muted. PhoneFinder rings through the phone’s dedicated alarm channel at full volume instead, the same way an alarm clock still wakes you when the phone is silent.

What you need

  • A Pebble Time 2 or Core Time 2 watch
  • The official Pebble app, with your watch paired
  • Both halves of PhoneFinder: the watchapp and the Android companion app

Good to know

Do Not Disturb set to total silence is enforced by Android itself, and no ordinary app can override it. In that mode PhoneFinder plays at whatever volume the system allows.
