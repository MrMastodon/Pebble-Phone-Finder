# Store listing copy

Ready to paste into Google Play Console and the Rebble developer portal.
Play's limits are title ≤ 30, short description ≤ 80, full description
≤ 4000 characters; `tools/check_listing_copy.py` verifies every field here
against them.

"PhoneFinder" is a brand name and stays untranslated in the Norwegian
listing — see `docs/RELEASE.md`.

> **On the title:** it's just "PhoneFinder", not "PhoneFinder for Pebble".
> Play prohibits using another brand in a way that implies affiliation, and
> while "for Pebble" is normally accepted as descriptive compatibility use,
> it does get flagged sometimes. Pebble appears in the short description
> instead, which still covers the search term without putting someone
> else's trademark in the app name. Use the longer title only if you're
> comfortable with that risk.

## Google Play — English (en-US)

### Title
```
PhoneFinder
```

### Short description
```
Ring your phone from your Pebble watch — loud, even when it's on silent.
```

### Full description
```
PhoneFinder rings your phone from your Pebble watch, so you can find it when it has slipped down the side of the sofa.

Most find-my-phone watchapps just play a media sound at whatever your volume happens to be set to — which is no help at all if your phone is muted. PhoneFinder plays through the phone's dedicated alarm channel at full volume instead. Silent mode and the ringer and media sliders don't affect it, the same way your alarm clock still wakes you when the phone is on silent.

WHAT YOU NEED

• A Pebble Time 2 or Core Time 2 watch
• The official Pebble app, with your watch paired
• The free PhoneFinder watchapp, installed on the watch from the Pebble appstore

This app on its own does nothing without the watchapp — it's the phone half of a pair.

HOW IT WORKS

• Open PhoneFinder on your watch and press the middle button
• Your phone starts ringing, loudly
• Press the button again to stop it, or tap Stop on the phone's notification

FEATURES

• A loud alarm that ignores silent mode and your volume settings
• Choose any of your phone's built-in alarm sounds, or keep the bundled one
• The watch screen shows whether it's currently connected to your phone
• Stop the alarm from either the watch or the phone
• Available in English and Norwegian

PRIVACY

PhoneFinder does not collect or share any user data. Nothing leaves your phone.

GOOD TO KNOW

Do Not Disturb set to total silence is enforced by Android itself, and no ordinary app can override it. In that mode PhoneFinder plays at whatever volume the system allows.

Some manufacturers are aggressive about closing background apps. If the alarm doesn't reliably fire, allow PhoneFinder to run unrestricted in your battery settings.
```

## Google Play — Norsk (nb-NO)

### Tittel

PhoneFinder

### Kort beskrivelse

Ring telefonen fra Pebble-klokken – høyt, selv når den står på lydløs.
```

### Fullstendig beskrivelse
```
PhoneFinder ringer opp telefonen din fra Pebble-klokken, så du finner den igjen når den har glidd ned bak sofaputa.

De fleste finn-telefonen-appene spiller bare av en medielyd på det volumet du tilfeldigvis har stilt inn — noe som ikke hjelper i det hele tatt hvis telefonen står på lydløs. PhoneFinder spiller i stedet gjennom telefonens egen alarmkanal på fullt volum. Lydløsmodus og volumknappene for ringelyd og medier påvirker den ikke, på samme måte som vekkerklokken fortsatt vekker deg når telefonen er lydløs.

DETTE TRENGER DU

• En Pebble Time 2 eller Core Time 2
• Den offisielle Pebble-appen, med klokken paret
• Den gratis PhoneFinder-appen installert på klokken fra Pebble-butikken

Denne appen gjør ingenting alene uten klokke-appen — den er telefonhalvdelen av et par.

SLIK VIRKER DET

• Åpne PhoneFinder på klokken og trykk på midtknappen
• Telefonen begynner å ringe, høyt
• Trykk på knappen igjen for å stoppe, eller trykk Stopp på varselet på telefonen

FUNKSJONER

• Høy alarm som overstyrer lydløsmodus og voluminnstillinger
• Velg hvilken som helst av telefonens innebygde alarmlyder, eller behold den medfølgende
• Klokkeskjermen viser om den er tilkoblet telefonen
• Stopp alarmen fra enten klokken eller telefonen
• Tilgjengelig på norsk og engelsk

PERSONVERN

PhoneFinder samler ikke inn eller deler noen brukerdata. Ingenting forlater telefonen din.

VERDT Å VITE

«Ikke forstyrr» satt til total stillhet håndheves av Android selv, og ingen vanlig app kan overstyre det. I den modusen spiller PhoneFinder på det volumet systemet tillater.

Enkelte produsenter er aggressive med å lukke apper i bakgrunnen. Hvis alarmen ikke utløses pålitelig, gi PhoneFinder lov til å kjøre uten begrensninger i batteriinnstillingene.
```

short/long split, and no documented length limit. Kept short and
watch-first, since someone browsing there already has the watch.

PhoneFinder
```

Lost your phone down the back of the sofa? Open PhoneFinder, press the middle button, and your phone rings — loudly, even if it's on silent.

Unlike find-my-phone apps that play a media sound at whatever volume happens to be set, PhoneFinder rings through the phone's alarm channel at full volume. Press the button again to stop it, or tap Stop on the notification that appears on the phone.

The watch screen shows whether it's currently connected to your phone, so you know before you press.

REQUIRES the free PhoneFinder companion app for Android, plus the official Pebble app with your watch paired. There is no iOS companion.

Built for the Pebble Time 2 / Core Time 2 (emery). English and Norwegian.

No data is collected or shared.
